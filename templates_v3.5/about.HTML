About

Paul-Josephs Video Game Information (PJ's VGI) is a website that stores the data of the worlds most popular games!

I chose to create a Webpage about games because video games have been a hobby of mine for a long time now, and I thought that it would be fun to create. The data in this webpage consists of the Game title, Series, Sale of the game, Developer, and Publisher. I am not the original owner of this data, The data was found on Kaggle and the original owner will be credited on the bottom of this page. One of the main purposes of PJ’s VGI would be to shine light on gaming and games, specifically for those who would be completely new to it all and are willing to experience and enjoy the gaming culture. My website would act as an introduction for all those people and would include just enough information to start or encourage the learning journey of the games on the website.

PJ’s VGIs target audience preferably would be English speaking children/teenagers and adults. Although my website would be accessible to many languages, I won't be making any translations so English would be the only language option. My website would be very welcoming to all kinds of viewers. Even if there were to be viewers with or without any gaming experience, PJ’s VGI would still have usefulness and understandability for both.
